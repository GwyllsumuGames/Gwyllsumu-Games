The Nightmare Begins

Set in the autumn of 1994, Blackwood Manor has stood abandoned since the tragic fire that claimed the lives of its final residents. You are an investigative journalist following a cryptic trail of letters left by your missing sister.

As you step into the decaying halls, you realize you are not alone. The whispers echoing through the corridors will push your sanity to the brink. To find the truth, you must first survive the dark.

Experience the Horror

Oppressive Atmosphere

Designed with advanced spatial audio and dynamic lighting to ensure you feel a constant, suffocating dread with every step you take.

Intricate Puzzles

Examine forgotten notes, decipher cryptic symbols, and unlock the hidden passages of the manor to uncover the grim reality.

Evade and Survive

You have no weapons. Your only options against the lurking entities are your wits, the shadows, and absolute silence.
