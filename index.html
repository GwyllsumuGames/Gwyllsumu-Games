01 // The Night Shift

Verify Before You Open

It is 2:00 AM during a campus-wide fog lockdown. You are locked inside your dormitory room with a reinforced steel door, a peephole, and a desk laptop connected directly to the university's student database.

Outside in the hallway, late-night visitors are asking to be let in. They know your friends' names, they wear their clothes, and they share their exact voices. But something doesn't add up.

Terminal Warning // Admin Bulletin
"Never unbolt the door based on a friendly voice alone. Check their last keycard swipe on your laptop screen. If the timestamp doesn't match their story, secure the deadbolt immediately."
02 // Core Routine

The Interrogation Protocol

Your survival during the night shift depends entirely on cold logic and attention to detail.

01

Search the Laptop

Cross-reference student records, keycard logs, and attendance sheets to catch hidden contradictions.

02

Speak Through the Door

Interrogate the person outside through the grate. Listen closely for awkward pauses, lies, or wrong answers.

03

Turn the Key

Admit genuine students seeking shelter, or trigger the security shutters before an Absentee slips inside.
