01 / Synopsis

The Echoes of Blackwood

Autumn, 1994. Northern Maine.

For twelve years, the charred remains of the Blackwood Estate have stood untouched behind police cordons. The official investigation concluded it was an accidental electrical fire. Your sister, an investigative archivist, disagreed—until she vanished inside the perimeter three days ago.

Recovered Audio Log — Tape #04
"The floorboards don't creak because they are old. They creak because something underneath is pressing up against them. Do not come looking for me."

Armed only with an analog camcorder and her unfinished research notes, you step past the rusted gates. As night falls, the architecture of the estate begins to defy logic, forcing you to confront the disturbing history of its former residents.

02 / Mechanics

Design Pillars

Built for tension, not cheap jump scares.

I.

Tactile Investigation

No floating quest markers or hand-holding UI. Physically inspect photographs, cross-reference medical records, and rewind VHS tapes to piece together the timeline and unlock sealed wings of the estate.

II.

Acoustic Survival

You cannot fight what haunts these halls. Listen closely to binaural 3D audio cues—creaking timber, distant breathing, and shifting debris—to anticipate movement and stay out of sight.

III.

A Shifting Reality

The environment reacts to your discoveries. Doors you just walked through may lead somewhere entirely different when you turn around, blurring the line between the manor's architecture and your own fracturing mind.
