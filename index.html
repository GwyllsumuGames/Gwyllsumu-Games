THE ABSENTEE

"The front desk ledger says one of your roommates signed out of the dormitory at 6:00 PM and never came back. Then who is sleeping in his bed right now?"

A thick winter fog has sealed St. Jude’s Dormitory. All three of your roommates are sitting inside Room 409 with you. They know your name, they wear your friends' clothes, and they share your memories. But one of them is an Absentee—something that walked in from the fog to fill an empty place.

01 // The Discrepancy

Count the Breathing Before You Close Your Eyes

When the campus power grid fails at midnight, the dormitory doors lock automatically until dawn. You are trapped in a forty-square-meter room with people you thought you knew.

The mimic does not attack when everyone is looking. It waits for you to trust it. It learns human habits in real time—adjusting its posture, copying vocal tics, and rehearsing answers. To survive the night, you must uncover who actually left the building before curfew, and confront the thing wearing their face.

Examine the Roommates

Click through the room logs below. Every occupant claims they never left the fourth floor tonight. Look closer at what doesn't match.

PROTOCOL // 01

Cross-Check the Lies

Compare what your roommates say against physical clues left in drawers, phone logs, and class schedules. An Absentee only knows what it has overheard in this room.

PROTOCOL // 02

Conceal Your Suspicion

If it realizes you have figured out its identity before you reach the door key, it stops pretending. Keep your tone calm and your questions casual.

PROTOCOL // 03

Make the Final Call

Before dawn, one person must be locked outside in the corridor. Choose carefully. If you exile your real friend, you lock yourself inside with the mimic.
