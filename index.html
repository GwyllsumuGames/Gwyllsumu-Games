01 / The Lockdown

The Fourth Breathing

4th Floor, East Hallway. Doors bolted.

According to university housing records, only three students are assigned to Room 409. Yet tonight, when the hallway lights died and the heavy fog swallowed the campus courtyard, you counted four distinct people breathing in the dark.

Voice Memo — Recorded Under the Blankets (02:14 AM)
"It copies his voice down to the slight cough when he laughs. It wears his clothes. It knows his hometown. But when I looked down from the top bunk ten minutes ago, I watched it practicing how to smile in the dark."

The campus phones are dead, and the resident advisor hasn't answered his door since sundown. Everyone in the room wears a familiar face, shares your classes, and swears they are human. But one of them forgets to blink when they think nobody is watching. Before morning comes, you must figure out who is wearing your friend's skin—because if you turn your back on the wrong one, it will be wearing yours next.

02 / Protocols

Dormitory Survival Rules

Found taped to the inside of the bathroom door.

I.

Study the Imperfections

They are convincing, but never flawless. A shirt buttoned wrong, a mole on the wrong cheek, a sudden inability to recall a conversation from yesterday, or a reflection that moves a fraction of a second too late. The truth hides in the details they failed to memorize.

II.

Question Without Alerting

Do not let them know you suspect them. Weave trap questions into casual conversation—ask about shared memories only the real student would know. If their eyes dart away or they repeat your question back to you, slowly step toward the door.

III.

Survive the Blackout

When the auxiliary generator fails at midnight, paranoia turns physical. You will have to choose who to trust, who to interrogate, and who to lock out in the hallway. Make the wrong call, and you will be locking yourself inside with it.

Logged Discrepancies

Things that do not add up in Room 409.

Discrepancy #01
The Lobby Sign-In Sheet

Everyone is sitting in the room with you right now. However, the security log downstairs shows one of your roommates signed out at 6:00 PM and never returned to the building.

Discrepancy #02
Fogged Bathroom Mirror

Someone left the hot water running. Beneath the condensation on the glass, a frantic message was traced from the inside: "IT HAS MY VOICE."

Discrepancy #03
The Duplicate Key

An extra brass key stamped with '409' was found under the study desk, covered in damp soil. All three roommates still have their keys on their lanyards.

Discrepancy #04
The Locked Wardrobe

Faint, desperate scratching sounds are coming from inside the heavy oak wardrobe in the corner—even though all of your roommates are standing right behind you.
