THE ABSENTEE

"Someone is knocking on the dormitory door, begging to be let in from the fog. They have your friend's face and student ID. But according to your laptop, your friend is already asleep down the hall."

It is 2:00 AM during a campus-wide lockdown. You are stationed behind a reinforced dormitory door with only a peephole, an intercom, and a laptop connected to the university's student records. Late-night stragglers are arriving at your door—and things wearing their faces are walking right behind them.

01 // The Screen & The Peephole

Cross-Reference Every Word Before You Turn the Lock

Between the hallway and safety stands a single steel door. Whenever footsteps stop outside, your routine is the same: look through the lens, speak through the grate, and search their name on your laptop.

Dig through class schedules, campus keycard swipes, medical files, and emergency emails. The doppelgängers outside know how to plead, lie, and imitate voices—but they cannot rewrite the digital trail left on your screen. Catch them in a lie before you slide the bolt back.

Campus Admin Alert Priority: CRITICAL
"If a resident claims they lost their ID card, check their last cafeteria swipe on your laptop. If the timestamp conflicts with their story, step away from the peephole and engage the deadbolt immediately."

STEP // 01

Search the Laptop

Access the university intranet, student dossiers, CCTV timestamps, and private emails. Every visitor leaves digital footprints—find out if their story holds up.

STEP // 02

Press for Details

Question them through the door. Ask about their classes, their roommates, or where they were when the lockdown started. Watch how their face reacts when you catch a contradiction.

STEP // 03

Admit or Lockdown

Turn the key for genuine students seeking shelter, or trigger the security shutters on the impostors. One mistake lets an Absentee into the dormitory.
